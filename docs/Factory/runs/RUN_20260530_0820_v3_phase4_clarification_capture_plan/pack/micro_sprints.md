## MS-01 Later Candidate Approval
- Objective: Obtain explicit future approval for `P4-NEG-CAPTURE-CANDIDATE-003`.

- Outputs: approval or no-go record naming future prompt, files, commands, telemetry decision, and dated result/profile IDs.

- Stop or go gate: stop if approval is ambiguous or candidate no longer fits `V3-OP-001` intake.

## MS-02 Future Clarification Intake

- Stop or go gate: stop before editing if target files, authority, commands, or verification are unclear.

- Entry criteria: MS-02 has source-derived or human-confirmed target files.

- Stop or go gate: stop on scope expansion, failed verification, or advisory-to-authority drift.

## MS-04 Future Capture Records
